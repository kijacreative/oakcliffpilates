Sessions are fully personalised: your instructor assesses your goals, mobility, and any injuries in the first session and builds a programme specifically for you.

02What to expect

What happens in a private session.

01 · The assessment

Your first session begins with a short assessment — your instructor will ask about your fitness background, any injuries or limitations, and what you want to achieve. This takes about 10 minutes and shapes everything that follows.

02 · The session itself

From there, every session is built around you: warm-up and mobility work on the reformer, the core movement sequence your programme prescribes, then strength or flexibility focus depending on your goals.

03 · The progression

Your programme evolves session to session. Unlike group classes where the instructor works with 12 people simultaneously, in a private your instructor's attention is entirely on your form, progression, and feedback.
