02Our journey

It started on the grass.

Oak Cliff Pilates was born out of a pure passion for community wellness and empowerment. It all began in 2016, when Amanda started offering Pilates in the park, fostering a close-knit community, five of whom are still active members today!

In June 2021, we took the significant leap and launched Oak Cliff Pilates as a formal studio. Our growth since then has been nothing short of extraordinary, but the core values of community, diversity, and quality have stayed the same.

03Our story

From one Dallas studio to three.

See the studios

Oak Cliff Pilates began with a simple mission: bring world-class reformer Pilates to Dallas neighborhoods. What started as a single studio in Bishop Arts has grown into three vibrant locations, each serving its unique Dallas community while maintaining the high-energy, results-driven approach that makes OCP special.

We're not just another "Pilates studio near me" – we're your neighbors, your accountability partners, and your biggest cheerleaders. Every reformer class, every personal training session, and every community event reflects our commitment to transforming lives through movement.
