Privacy
policy

Effective 1 November 2024

Oak Cliff Pilates (OCP) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

a. Personal information

When you sign up for classes, make a purchase, or subscribe to our mailing list, we may collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing address
  • Payment information

b. Usage data

When you visit our website, we may collect information about your device, browsing actions, and patterns, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visit

c. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, analyze usage data, and deliver targeted advertisements. You can see exactly what this website stores, and change your choices at any time, in cookie preferences.

2. How we use your information

  • To process transactions: we use your payment information to complete purchases.
  • To communicate with you: we send emails and text messages about upcoming classes, promotions, and important updates.
  • To improve our website and services: usage data helps us analyze how our site is used and identify areas for improvement.
  • To personalize your experience: we may use your information to tailor our offerings based on your interests and preferences.
  • For marketing purposes: with your consent, we may use your information to inform you about events, offers, and services that might be of interest to you.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your information with trusted partners and service providers to help us operate our business, such as:

  • Payment processors: to securely process payments.
  • Email and SMS providers: to communicate with you about bookings, updates, and offers.
  • Marketing platforms: to provide you with personalized marketing and advertisements, with your consent.
  • Legal compliance: if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of OCP or others.

4. Your choices and rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: you can request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: you can request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: you can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to legal requirements.
  • Opt out of marketing: you can unsubscribe from our marketing emails or texts at any time by following the unsubscribe link in any communication.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@oakcliffpilates.com.

5. Security of your information

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on our website. Your continued use of our website or services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Oak Cliff Pilates
info@oakcliffpilates.com
oakcliffpilates.com

Thank you for trusting Oak Cliff Pilates with your personal information. We value your privacy and are dedicated to safeguarding your information.
