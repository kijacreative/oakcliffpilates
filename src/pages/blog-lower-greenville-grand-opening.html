Lower Greenville grand opening

November 4, 20251 min read

Our Lower Greenville studio officially opened its doors on September 21st, and we couldn’t be more grateful for the love this community has shown.

The grand opening was an unforgettable day filled with classes, giveaways, local vendors, music, and plenty of OCP energy. The new studio features 14 reformers, private training space, retail apparel, and our new podcast studio, marking the next exciting chapter for Oak Cliff Pilates.

Thank you to everyone who joined us for our biggest milestone yet. We’re just getting started.
