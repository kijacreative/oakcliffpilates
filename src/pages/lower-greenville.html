03Visit us

Getting here & parking.

Get directions

The studio is located at 2000 Greenville Ave, Dallas, TX 75206, near the corner of Greenville Avenue and Prospect Avenue. The easiest parking is in the lot right next to the studio on Oram St., with street parking and neighbouring lots as backup — check for “do not park” signs.

From Lakewood or the M Streets, it's approximately a five-minute drive. If you're coming from downtown Dallas, take US-75 North and exit at Knox-Henderson for the quickest route.
