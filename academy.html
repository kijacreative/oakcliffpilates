03The curriculum

What you will learn.

01 · Anatomy & movement principles

Develop a strong understanding of anatomy, biomechanics, posture, and movement patterns to build safe, effective, and confident Pilates instruction.

02 · Reformer repertoire

Learn the complete Reformer exercise library, including progressions, regressions, and proper exercise sequencing.

03 · Teaching methodology

Develop cueing techniques, class planning skills, and teaching strategies for clients of different experience levels.

04 · Injury awareness

Understand common injuries, movement modifications, and safe programming to support a wide range of clients.

05 · Practicum experience

Gain real-world teaching experience through supervised practicum sessions with structured mentor feedback.

Are you down with OCP?

We use a couple of cookies.

Two small ones, both ours: one remembers this choice, one remembers you have already seen the intro offer. Booking and checkout run on Arketa and set their own. No analytics, no advertising, nothing that follows you to other sites.

Cookie preferences

Strictly necessary Always on

The site cannot work without these, so there is no switch. None of them identify you or leave this site.

  • ocp_consent — remembers what you choose here. 12 months.
  • ocp_popup_seen — so the intro offer shows once, not on every page. 30 days.
  • Arketa — our booking and payment system, embedded on the schedule, shop and checkout pages. It sets its own cookies so it can hold your basket and keep you signed in. Blocking them would mean you could not book or pay.
Video

Videos in the Journal are served by YouTube. Nothing loads until you press play, and we use their no-cookie domain — but pressing play still contacts Google, which may store data. Leave this off and we will ask before any video loads.
