01 — Run of show

The 45-minute standard

Every class is 45 minutes, start to finish. Here's what that looks like from the moment the door opens to the moment they walk out.

−[15] minDoors

Be on the floor before they are

Room set, springs reset, music on, lights right. You're greeting people as they walk in — not still setting up.

  • Learn who's new before class starts. Check the roster.
  • Greet every client by name. New or 200 classes in.
−[8] minNew clients

Walk new clients through the reformer

Never let someone's first class start cold. Pull them to a reformer and cover it hands-on.

  • Carriage, footbar, headrest, shoulder blocks — what moves and what doesn't.
  • Springs and resistance: how you'll call them, how they change them.
  • Straps and handles, safe entry and exit off the carriage.
  • Then ask: "Any questions before we start?" Answer them before you cue the first movement.
0:00 — 45:00Class

Teach the class that's on the schedule

If it says [Sculpt], teach [Sculpt]. Clients book by class type and they should get exactly what they signed up for — every time, at every location, from every trainer.

  • Start on time and end on time. 45 minutes means 45 minutes.
  • Cue for the room you actually have — offer the modification and the progression.
  • Move through the room. Hands-on corrections where welcome, eyes on new clients throughout.
  • Use names. That's the whole difference between a workout and our workout.
45:00The close

Don't skip the last two minutes

How you end class is how they remember it. Every class closes the same four ways:

  • Thank the class. Out loud, to the whole room, before anyone gets up.
  • Ask for the review. "If you liked today, leave us a Google review — it's how people find us." [Link/QR at the front desk.]
  • Answer questions. Stay on the floor a few minutes. Don't disappear into the back.
  • Talk memberships. First-timers and anyone on an intro pack get asked what's next. Know the current offers below cold.
Non-negotiables
45 minutesStart on time. End on time. No 40, no 50.
Teach the typeThe schedule is a promise. Deliver what it says.
Nobody starts coldEvery new client gets the reformer intro.

05 — On the floor

Tips

The three things new trainers ask about most. Learn them once and the desk stops being a bottleneck.

Tip 01

How to use Arketa

Arketa is where the roster, the check-ins, and the client history live. Open it before you unlock the door, not after class starts.

  1. Log in at [app.arketa.co] with your trainer account. Ask [Charley] if you don't have one.
  2. Go to Schedule and open today's class at your location.
  3. Pull up the roster — first-timers are flagged. Note their names before anyone walks in.
  4. Tap a client to see class count, membership, and notes. Injuries and modifications live here.
  5. Leave a note after class when something matters for the next trainer.

If Arketa is down, take attendance on paper and send it to [Charley] the same day.

Tip 02

Checking out clients

Everyone in the room gets checked in before you cue the first movement. An unchecked client is an unpaid class.

  1. Open today's class in Schedule and tap Check in next to each name as they arrive.
  2. Walk-in with no booking? Add them to the class, then take payment or apply their pass at the desk.
  3. No credits left on their account: sell the [3 Classes Intro] or a class pack right in Arketa before class.
  4. No-shows and late cancels stay unchecked — don't check in someone who isn't in the room.
  5. Close out the roster before you leave. Every name marked one way or the other.

Billing disputes go to [Kiel] — never comp or refund on the spot.

Tip 03

Moving clients in class

Reformer swaps happen constantly — a spring issue, a height mismatch, a client who wants the back row. Move them, then fix the record.

  1. Ask before you move anyone. "Do you mind sliding down one?" — never reassign silently.
  2. Update the spot assignment on the roster so the reformer number matches the person on it.
  3. Broken equipment: move the client first, log the reformer number the same day, and flag the spot so it can't be booked.
  4. New clients belong where you can see them — front row or nearest the door.
  5. Wrong class or wrong time? Move their booking in Arketa instead of sending them to the desk.

Anything you change mid-class, note it on the roster before you clock out.
