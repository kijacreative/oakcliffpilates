Best Pilates Studio in Dallas. Again.

September 17, 20251 min read

Dallas Observer Critics’ Choice 2025.

We’re thrilled to share that Oak Cliff Pilates has been named the Best Pilates Studio in Dallas by the Dallas Observer — for the third year in a row.

This recognition means so much because it celebrates what we’ve built together: a community that moves with strength, intention, and joy. The Critics’ Choice award highlights not only the quality of our instruction and training but also the energy that fills our studios every day.

When we started Oak Cliff Pilates, our mission was simple: to make Pilates approachable, empowering, and rooted in community. Winning this honor again is proof that staying true to that mission continues to make an impact across Dallas.

Thank you to our amazing instructors, dedicated members, and every new client who’s stepped onto a reformer with us this year.
